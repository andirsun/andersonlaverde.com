![The post in Slack, under my name with the 5 years badge.](slack.png)

On a world changing fast because of AI, I sometimes found myself kinda anxious or left behind by the industry. I took a moment yesterday to think about companies are made by humans and as humans we are more than intelligence, yeah sure… there are processes that can be automatized and business decisions that can be improved using AI but days like yesterday made me think that even the world is changing, humans will always remain and adapt.

I am about to complete 5 years here and over the years I learnt a lot of things about how humans can think on a single problem and find solutions. Problems → discussions → solutions → results, this is a human process not aiming for perfection but iteration. Life is at the end just a process not a result.

Glad to be here sharing this with a team full of real and talented humans 💚
